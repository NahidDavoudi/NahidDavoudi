## 👋 About

I'm a self-directed software developer based in Babol, Iran, and a civil engineering student at Babol Noshirvani University of Technology. I build and ship real software: I founded **NadStore**, a custom e-commerce platform now serving three active businesses, and I manage digital and IT operations at **Famo Academy**.

Outside of code, I write about economics, political philosophy and social issues, and I'm learning German.

## 🚀 What I'm working on

- **NadStore**: a custom e-commerce platform for small and medium-sized businesses, built on **NadCore**, my own PHP MVC framework.
- **NadCore**: simplifying and stabilizing it for production client use.
- **Famo**: rebuilding an institute management system on a cleaner architecture.
- Moving more of my stack toward **TypeScript** and **PostgreSQL**.
